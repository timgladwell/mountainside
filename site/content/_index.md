      Adam has been designing houses in Collingwood, the Blue Mountains,

      architectural designer, not an architect; the distinction matters,
      and he's precise about it.
    - >
      There are two different jobs in a project like this. One is bringing
      enough experience to help a client see what actually fits — where
      the light lands, how a family really uses a kitchen, what a lot
      allows before anyone draws a line. The other is turning what a
      client already knows they want into drawings, a permit, and
      something a builder can build without guessing. Adam keeps those two
      jobs separate on purpose. He'll push, when there's time to, if he
      thinks there's a better answer. But the work is getting a client's
      house built, not talking them into his own.
    - >
      Before Mountainside Design, Adam spent several years working at
      other design firms in the area. Running his own practice since 2015
      means every project gets him directly — the first site walk, the
      drawings and the revisions, the town's approvals, and the site
      visit when something has to change today because the concrete
      truck is already on the road. There's plenty of design talent in
      this area already; Adam isn't trying to out-market it. Most of the
      work still comes from people who were told to call him.
